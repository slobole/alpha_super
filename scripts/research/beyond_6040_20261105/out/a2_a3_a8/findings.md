# A2 / A3 / A8 findings (fixed engine; dev sample 1970-2022; ETF era 2005-2022; excess Sharpe over Fed funds; B0 = 0.428 full / 0.796 ETF, max DD -16.2%, turnover 1.8/yr)

**A2 weighting - nothing to adopt; inverse-vol stays.** Equal weights: Sharpe 0.516 full / 0.750 ETF; the gain is one decade (1970s 0.80 vs 0.13, gold at a third); worst block (1980s) -0.03 vs -0.01, ETF-era bootstrap p05 -0.13 -> fails (a) and (c). IVOL/EW blend: 0.480 / 0.780; passes on full history by a hair (worst block -0.008 vs -0.010, p05 -0.006) but loses 0.016 in the ETF era with p05 -0.06 -> not adopted. ERC: 0.373 / 0.719, Sharpe down 0.055, turnover 1.9x -> fails. MinVar: 0.337 / 0.857, worst block -0.26 (1970s), max DD -19.6%, turnover 2.3x -> fails. Concentration (max weight of the risky sleeve > 60%): MinVar 85% of days (avg bond 55%), ERC 22%, IVOL 9%, equal/blend 0%. Average IVOL weights stock/bond/gold 31/41/29%.

**A3 estimator - faster estimators look a little better in the ETF era, but inside bootstrap noise; keep rolling 63 pending A1.** Full Sharpe 0.424-0.451 across rolling 21/63/126/252, EWMA(0.94), max(21,63); ETF era 0.755-0.837. Rolling-21: 0.451 / 0.837, max DD -15.2%, passes the adoption rule in both windows (bootstrap median +0.02 / +0.04, p05 -0.01 / -0.02) at 1.7x turnover (3.1/yr). EWMA: 0.424 / 0.821, worst block -0.065 -> fails. max(21,63): 0.440 / 0.822, DD -15.4%, worst block -0.03 -> fails (a). Rolling 126/252: +0.015-0.02 full Sharpe but DD -17.2% / -19.3% and fail (c) in the ETF era. The 63-day cell sits in the middle of a flat plateau; the window dimension of A1 should decide whether 21 is a real centre or a corner.

**The overlay itself is insurance, not alpha.** Inverse-vol with no vol overlay: Sharpe 0.431 full / 0.760 ETF (vs 0.428 / 0.796), CAGR 8.5% vs 8.0%, but max DD -22.5% vs -16.2% and worst 12m -18.7% vs -13.0%. The 8% target costs ~0.5% CAGR per year, cuts max DD by 28%, and adds Sharpe only in the ETF era (+0.04).

**A8 leverage - Sharpe falls after financing, as expected; not worth it for Growth.** Sanity: max_exposure=1.0 reproduces B0 exactly (identical daily returns). Uncapped, full history: 1.25x Sharpe 0.399, vol 8.0%, CAGR 8.1%, max DD -19.0%; 1.5x Sharpe 0.394, vol 8.4%, CAGR 8.2%, DD -22.6%, worst 12m -17.5%; 2.0x Sharpe 0.400, vol 8.7%, CAGR 8.3%, DD -26.5%. Financing (Fed funds + 1.5% on the borrowed part) costs 0.9% / 1.3% / 1.7% of NAV per year; the engine's excess return is ~2.9%/yr, so each unit of borrowed exposure nets ~1.4% before the extra drawdown. ETF era: Sharpe 0.78-0.79 at every leverage level vs 0.796 unlevered. With the author's cap (1.5x total, 0.7 per asset): 1.5x Sharpe 0.424, CAGR 8.4%, DD -16.6%; 2.0x Sharpe 0.436, CAGR 8.6%, vol 8.3%, DD -16.6%, worst 12m -14.8%, bootstrap diff median +0.01 (p05 -0.035). Continuous overlay (exposure = min(cap, 8%/vol) every day) adds another +0.008 Sharpe (1.5x 0.432, 2x 0.444; ETF 0.796 / 0.794), DD -16.5%, but turnover 3.8-4.0/yr = 2.1-2.3x B0 -> fails (d). The best levered cell (2x capped) is +0.6% CAGR for +1.2pp vol and 0.4pp deeper DD, Sharpe +0.01 - inside noise. All twelve levered variants fail adoption: the 1980s block falls (-0.03 to -0.05 vs -0.01) and the 1970s falls uncapped (0.04 vs 0.13). Average exposure 1.07 / 1.14 / 1.19 (1.25x / 1.5x / 2x); exposure > 1.5 on 7% of days at 1.5x and 20% at 2x uncapped.

**Does 1.5x hit 8%?** Roughly: realised vol 8.4% full / 8.5% ETF, median rolling-63 vol 8.0% (B0 6.9%), above 10% on 10% of days (B0 4%) and above 12% on 4%. Leverage makes the vol target symmetric and therefore noisier, not tighter.

**Leverage path in engine.run - works after the fix.** Daily exposure changes now trade (B0 turnover 1.1 -> 1.8/yr), and the dead-band snaps mostly went away: B0 has 0 one-day exposure jumps > 0.3 (max 0.26); 1.5x uncapped 8 (max 0.74, 1973-01-05 1.50 -> 0.76), 2x uncapped 10 (max 1.23), capped 5. continuous_overlay=True does NOT remove the rest: 4 jumps at 1.5x and 2x (max 0.61 / 0.74). Those are the 63-day estimator reacting to a single large day entering the window (1973-01, 1975-10, 1979-10, 2013-04 gold), amplified by leverage, not a band artefact. Realised gross exposure still edges above the cap through intraday drift (1.53 at cap 1.5; 2.06 at cap 2.0), which is cosmetic.
