# A12 Book fit: Beyond 60/40 (B0) next to the house pods

Window 2008-03-04 -> 2022-12-30 (3,735 sessions; TAA on its proxy run before 2012-10-02, so the 2012-> cut is shown beside it). Sharpe = excess over Fed funds. Pods carry the fund study's fair-cash add; B0 earns Fed funds on idle cash, 5 bp cost. Engine with the vol-overlay trading fix of 2026-11-05.

**Verdict: B0 neither replaces CORE5 nor earns a slot beside it. It adds nothing a book does not already have.**

1. B0 vs CORE5 head to head: B0 6.3%/yr, Sharpe 0.76, max DD -16.2%, worst 12m -14.5%. CORE5 5.4%/yr, Sharpe 0.82, max DD -6.8%, worst 12m -4.1%. Correlation 0.53 daily, 0.56 monthly. A 50/50 blend gives Sharpe 0.90 but max DD -10.0% and worst 12m -6.2%: a little more Sharpe for half again the drawdown.
2. B0 is mostly a 60/40 beta sleeve: monthly correlation 0.70 with 60/40, 0.45-0.61 with the TAA pods, 0.11-0.13 with the MR capsule and the Flow Vote. CORE5's correlation with 60/40 is 0.29.
3. Stress, months when stocks and the long bond both fell (26 of 178): B0 -2.3% a month, -45.1% cumulative, up in 8% of them. CORE5 -0.4% a month, -9.5% cumulative. 60/40 -4.1% a month. Flow Vote +1.2% a month, MR capsule flat. 2022: B0 -12.1%, CORE5 +4.0%, 60/40 -23.3%.
4. The SPY-TLT 120-day correlation never closed a month above 0.3 in 2008-2022 (max 0.24, Dec 2022), so that cut is empty. In the 11 months with correlation above 0 (taper 2013, mid-2021, Aug-Dec 2022): B0 +0.4% in total, CORE5 +3.6%, TAA 1N +33.6%, MR capsule +28.2%. In the top-quintile months (36): B0 Sharpe 0.08, CORE5 1.16.
5. Defensive book (CORE5 54 / BTAL_QQQ 36 / BIL 10): as is Sharpe 1.00, max DD -5.6%, worst 12m -3.5%. B0 at 20% pro rata: Sharpe 1.02, DD -6.6%, worst 12m -4.3%. At 33%: 1.01, -7.9%, -4.9%. B0 in CORE5's place: 0.97, -10.3%, -5.9%; on 2012-> Sharpe 0.81 vs 1.05. Half of CORE5's weight to B0: 1.03, -7.4%; on 2012-> 0.96 vs 1.05. Every variant deepens the drawdown; the Sharpe gains (0.01-0.03) are noise and turn negative on 2012->.
6. Growth book (TAA 3x 40 / NDX caps 30 / MR 30): as is 17.1%/yr, Sharpe 1.24, DD -13.0%. B0 at 20%: 15.1%/yr, 1.26, -11.7%; at 33%: 13.8%/yr, 1.26, -11.5%. On 2012-> Sharpe falls 1.41 -> 1.38 -> 1.35. That is dilution: 2-3 points of return a year for 1.3-1.5 points of drawdown.
7. Growth Plus (TAA 1N 3x 40 / NDX caps 30 / MR 30) is the book that benefits most, and it is still small: Sharpe 1.16 -> 1.18 -> 1.19 at 20% and 33%, DD -16.3% -> -15.1% -> -14.3%, worst 12m -12.1% -> -10.2% -> -9.2%, at a cost of 2.4-4.0 points of return a year; on 2012-> Sharpe falls 1.29 -> 1.27 -> 1.25.
8. One pod per engine: B0 and CORE5 are the same engine (stocks / long bond / gold, risk-weighted, volatility-controlled). CORE5 does that job with 40% of B0's drawdown and a positive 2022, so B0 cannot join a book that holds CORE5, and in the growth books it is a lower-Sharpe, higher-correlation stand-in for a defensive sleeve.

What B0 is good for inside the house: a long-history (1970->) benchmark and sanity floor for CORE5-type rules, and the base for the leverage arm A8 if a Growth-shelf variant is ever wanted. Not a pod.

Caveats: single development window, no bootstrap on the differences (all within +/-0.05 Sharpe anyway); TAA history before 2012-10 is the proxy run; the MR capsule here is the liquidity-sized version (two pods, monthly 50/50), the Growth books hold the BIL-parked version per their YAMLs.
Tables: corr_daily.csv, corr_monthly.csv, pods_standalone.csv, books_marginal.csv, b0_vs_core5.csv, b0_core5_by_year.csv, stress_stock_bond_corr.csv, sources.txt.

## T1 (trend-tilted)

T1 = B0 plus a per-asset 10-month SMA tilt (weight x 0.5 or 1.0, set monthly), read from out/a13/series_daily.parquet (fixed engine). Same window 2008-03-04 -> 2022-12-30, same conventions; tables t1_*.csv.

**Verdict: T1 is a real improvement on B0 and the right shape of engine, but it does not beat CORE5 for the defensive slot. It joins the house as CORE5's long-history validation, not as a second pod.**

1. T1 vs CORE5 head to head: T1 5.7%/yr, Sharpe 0.79, max DD -7.8%, worst 12m -6.6%, worst month -3.5%. CORE5 5.4%/yr, Sharpe 0.82, max DD -6.8%, worst 12m -4.1%. On 2012->: T1 Sharpe 0.57 vs CORE5 0.94 (4.0% vs 5.4% a year). Correlation 0.64 daily, 0.61 monthly (B0-T1 0.80 monthly; T1-60/40 0.48, down from B0's 0.70).
2. The tilt fixes B0's stress problem by half: both-down months (26) T1 -1.3% a month, -28.8% cumulative, up in 19% of them (B0 -2.3%/-45.1%, CORE5 -0.4%/-9.5%, 60/40 -4.1%). 2022: T1 -6.3%, B0 -12.1%, CORE5 +4.0%, 60/40 -23.3%. In the other 152 months T1 trails B0 (0.78% vs 1.02% a month): the insurance costs about 0.7% a year of CAGR, inside the spec's limit once the drawdown cut is counted.
3. 50/50 T1+CORE5 (monthly): Sharpe 0.89, max DD -5.8%, worst 12m -4.3%; better Sharpe than either and a shallower drawdown than CORE5 alone. On 2012-> the blend gives 0.83 vs CORE5's 0.94, so the gain is all 2008-2012, where TAA is also on a proxy.
4. Defensive book: as is Sharpe 1.00 / DD -5.6% / worst 12m -3.5%. T1 at 20%: 1.01 / -5.5% / -3.6%; at 33%: 1.00 / -5.6% / -3.8%; T1 in CORE5's place: 0.98 / -6.6% / -4.7% (2012->: 0.85 vs 1.05); half of CORE5's weight to T1: 1.02 / -5.7% / -3.8% (2012->: 0.97 vs 1.05). Unlike B0, T1 no longer deepens the book's drawdown, but it never improves it either, and every 2012-> cut loses 0.04-0.20 Sharpe.
5. Growth: 17.1%/yr, 1.24, DD -13.0% as is; T1 at 20%: 15.0%, 1.26, -10.8%, worst 12m -11.5% -> -9.3%; at 33%: 13.5%, 1.26, -10.0%, -7.8%. 2012-> Sharpe 1.41 -> 1.39 -> 1.36. Growth Plus: 19.2%, 1.16, -16.3% as is; 20%: 16.6%, 1.18, -14.2%; 33%: 14.9%, 1.19, -12.9%, worst 12m -12.1% -> -7.9%; 2012-> 1.29 -> 1.28 -> 1.26. T1 cuts the growth books' drawdowns by 2-3 points where B0 cut 1.3-2.0, for the same 2-4 points of return a year. Growth Plus benefits most.
6. One pod per engine: T1 and CORE5 are now the same engine in form as well as assets (risk-weighted stocks / long bond / gold, SMA10 per sleeve, volatility control), correlation 0.6, and CORE5 wins on every 2012-> measure by a margin that is not noise (0.37 Sharpe). T1 does not get a slot while CORE5 holds the defensive seat.
7. Where T1's 1970+ history matters: CORE5 is validated on 2007-> only (DBC/UUP start 2006-07), one rate cycle with a bond bull market. T1 proves the engine family's mechanism (inverse vol + vol target + SMA tilt) across the 1970s inflation, 1987, and the 1994 and 2022 bond falls, which is the evidence CORE5 cannot produce for itself. That is a reason to raise confidence in CORE5's slot and to document T1's long-history record as CORE5's family caveat, not a reason to run both. If CORE5 ever fails its own review, T1 is the pre-validated successor for the slot, and a leveraged T1 (A8) is the only form that could serve the growth books, where a defensive sleeve at 1x only dilutes.
