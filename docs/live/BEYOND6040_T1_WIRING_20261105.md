## Frozen strategy contract

- Strategy `strategies.taa_beyond_6040.strategy_taa_beyond_6040_trend_tilt`, default config only. Zero variants
  were searched for the wiring.
- Assets: VTI, TLT, GLD risky; BIL reserve. Long only, no leverage.
- Decision at Close_T from end-of-day data, every exchange session: `compute_signals` over observed prices through T,
  read at row T. Month-end rows set the base inverse-63-day-vol weights (CAPITALSPECIAL closes) and the 10-month SMA
  tilt multipliers (TOTALRETURN closes, ×1 above, ×0.5 below); every row sets the exposure from the 63-day realised
  vol of the base sleeve (8% target, 8.5% trigger). Final risky weight_i = exposure × base_i × m_i; BIL takes the
  residual once it has a Close_T (2007-05-30), cash before.
- `NAV_T = EOD cash + Σ(held shares × Close_T)`, CAPITALSPECIAL Close_T. `target_i = trunc(NAV_T × w_i / Close_T_i)`;
  `delta_i = target_i − held_i`. No later quote or NAV resizes these shares.
- Orders: MKT/OPG at the next open (`next_open_moo`), as CORE5.
- Data profile `norgate_eod_beyond6040`: unpadded observed rows from 2001-05-31 through T. CAPITALSPECIAL VTI, TLT,
  GLD, BIL; TOTALRETURN VTI, TLT, GLD, `$SPX`, `$SPXTR`. The live frame equals the research frame.

```text
[Close_T: norgate_eod_beyond6040 snapshot + strict read of the dedicated account]
                             |
[compute_signals through T, row T: base weights, tilt, exposure -> full target weight book incl. BIL]
                             |
     *** CRITICAL *** frozen trunc(NAV_T * w / Close_T) shares; no later price resizes them
                             |
[T+1, open - 6m30s: VPlan + fresh strict read (holdings, every client's open orders); block at open - 2 min]
                             |
[MKT/OPG -> fills -> daily settlement from fresh holdings; receipt advances only if actual = frozen]
```

## Changes to the strategy module made by the wiring

- **BIL reserve** (decision taken here): the residual goes to BIL, as CORE5. The research credited Fed funds on idle
  cash; BIL is its tradable form. House backtest 2005-10 → 2026-10: CAGR 5.56% → 5.81%, max DD −9.32% → −8.88%.
- **Trade/no-trade test on Close_T** (review finding): the engine skipped an asset when `floor(NAV×w/Open_(T+1))`
  equalled the shares held, while sizing the order from Close_T. Live cannot know Open_(T+1), so on 14% of sessions
  live would have sent orders the backtest never made. The skip test now uses Close_T, the price that sizes the
  order. Effect: CAGR 5.81% → 5.78%, max DD unchanged at −8.88%.
- **Data window** starts at VTI's first session (2001-05-31) so the live and research frames are the same; the
  first signal is still in 2005 (GLD), and the pre-BIL numbers reproduce the research replay exactly.

## Known caveats (on record, not fixed)

- **Daily churn.** The book re-sizes every session (NAV and exposure move daily): about 580 small orders a year, ~6×
  turnover, most under $1,000 at a $100K pod. Negligible in bps at fund scale; at a small account the $1 IBKR minimum
  adds up. A 5% exposure band was free in research (A1) and is the obvious next execution rule; it needs its own parity.
- **IBKR cross-cancel.** T1 sends GLD, TLT and BIL MOO orders most days; CORE5 trades GLD and BIL. Related accounts can
  hit "would cross related resting order" (incident 2026-11-02). The pod catches up the next session.
- **Accrued dividends.** Live NAV uses TotalCashValue, which excludes declared-not-paid dividends; between ex-date and
  pay date targets can be a share lower, then trade back. Not visible to the parity proof (which feeds engine cash).
- **No cash buffer.** BIL takes the whole residual; an opening gap can leave a small debit (backtest: 182 days, worst
  −0.84% of NAV). Margin accounts absorb it; a cash account would reject the leg and catch up next session.
- **Shared reconcile sells BIL after a failed send** (house rule for every daily pod, CORE5 included): if a claimed batch
  never went out, the daily reconcile sells the BIL excess over target in the session while the buys wait for the
  next decision. Left unchanged (shared live path).
- **Long data history required.** The replay needs every XNYS session since 2001 for each pair; a missing Norgate bar
  blocks the pod until the data is fixed.
