  The bias is conservative and, since the adjustment factors shrink toward recent years, weighs most on the early
  years. It is corrected here only in a separate "commission-fixed" table; the engine fix is a separate task.

## Part A: the 2008 proxy

The BTAL TAA sleeves start 2012-10-02 (TQQQ launched in 2010, BTAL in 2011). Before that date, the old tables used
`taa_1n_qld`: 1/N rules, four defensive assets (no BTAL) and a 2x fallback. The new proxy runs the real strategy
code through the real engine over 2008. It serves synthetic TQQQ and BTAL only for the dates before each fund
existed.

**Synthetic leveraged ETFs.** `r = L * r_underlying - (L - 1) * T-bill - drag`, with the drag fitted on 2010-2026
only.
- **Fit:** TQQQ's in-sample daily correlation is 0.9987.
- **Out of sample:** QLD over the GFC window returned -77.2%, against -78.1% for the real fund. Calendar years
  differ by 0.2-1.8 pp, all in the synthetic's favour (about 1-2 pp a year optimistic).
- **Failed bar:** the pre-declared daily-correlation bar (0.99) failed narrowly (QLD 0.987, SPXL 0.990). It was
  accepted after the results (A2).
- **Financing:** a +3%/yr financing stress on synthetic TQQQ before 2010 (for 2008's LIBOR spread) moves every
  book's drawdown by at most 0.2 pp.

**Synthetic BTAL.** Long the lowest-beta fifth and short the highest-beta fifth of Russell 1000 members, within
GICS sectors (252-day beta vs SPY, monthly rebalance, drifting in between), plus T-bill interest on the collateral.
- **Fit:** monthly correlation with real BTAL is 0.935 (daily 0.821).
- **Amplitude:** the raw replica runs larger than BTAL. Scaled (k = 0.734, fitted on daily data over 2011-2026)
  and unscaled (k = 1) are reported as a bracket:
  - Daily data understate BTAL's beta (stale closes): monthly beta -0.66 vs daily -0.50.
  - The fair scale is probably about 0.85.
- **Direction:** the scaled version is the conservative end for 2008. Book drawdowns differ by at most 0.4 pp
  between the two.
- **Independent check:** an S&P 500 Low Volatility minus High Beta series corroborates the path, GFC +44% against
  +35% (scaled) and +51% (unscaled).

**Strategy-level check (decisive).** Each TAA sleeve was re-run on synthetic instruments for its whole history,
then compared with the real run on 2012-10 -> 2026-08:

| Sleeve | Monthly corr | CAGR synthetic / real | Max DD synthetic / real | Allocation overlap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAA 3x rank (`taa_btal_tqqq`) | 0.975 | 24.4% / 23.8% | -17.6% / -17.7% | 96.6% |
| TAA 3x 1/N (`taa_btal_1n_tqqq`) | 0.985 | 31.6% / 30.3% | -25.4% / -25.5% | 97.5% |
| BTAL_QQQ (`taa_btal_lin_qqq`) | 0.965 | 13.2% / 12.7% | -10.2% / -11.1% | 96.3% |

All pass: correlation >= 0.90, CAGR within 2 pp, drawdown within 5 pp. The synthetic runs are 0.5-1.3 pp a year
optimistic, about 0.3 pp less once commissions are fixed, since the synthetic TQQQ traded at a higher adjusted price.

**What the proxy shows for 2008-09.** The TAA 3x rank sleeve held 0-7% TQQQ through the GFC; the 1/N sleeve held
up to 20% (May-Sep 2008). The rank sleeve lost only 4% in the GFC window. Its -26% drawdown from July 2008 to July
2009 splits as:
- DBC: -10.1 pp.
- TQQQ: -7.1 pp. TQQQ also set the trough, -8.4 pp over 1-7 July 2009, after the sleeve fell back into it.
- GLD: -3.8 pp.
- Phantom commissions: -3.2 pp.
- TLT: -1.4 pp.
- BTAL: -1.1 pp net.

### Ease of trading and capacity

**Method.** Capacity uses the last three years of fills and the house route model.
- **Fund route:** monthly orders are worked over up to 5 days, and BTAL / UUP / DBC trade as blocks.
- **Short-hold orders:** stock orders go to the close auction; industry-ETF DV2 orders are worked within one day.
- **Recommended size:** the largest size at which every gate is met and cost stays at or below 25% of the return
  over T-bills.

| Book | Pods | Trading days/yr | WIRED share | MOO today | MOC | Fund route | Cost at $25M | Missing before live |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G3 | 2 | 12 | 100% | $1M | $2.5M | $100M | 0.67%/yr | nothing |
| G4 | 3 | 17 | 67% | $0.5M | $2.5M | $50M | 0.89%/yr | MOSAIC wiring |
| G3 + capsule | 5 | 241 | 88% | $2.5M | $5M | $10M | 1.00%/yr | industry-ETF DV2 wiring |
| ladder_4 (drift) | 5 | 232 | 92% | $2.5M | $5M | $10M | 1.07%/yr | MOSAIC wiring |
| ladder_4_1n (drift) | 5 | 232 | 92% | $2.5M | $2.5M | $10M | 0.88%/yr | MOSAIC wiring |

Every MR book stops at $10M on the fund route. At $25M both short-hold stock pods break the close-auction limit
(P95 0.25%, P99 0.50% of daily volume). In G3 + capsule, DV2 is at P95 0.31% / P99 0.52% (41 orders over the
hard limit) and HPI at 0.27% / 0.46% (14 orders). Growing past $10M needs liquidity floors on both. The DV2
floor/ADV modules exist; HPI has none.

## Recommendation (judgement)

1. **Now: G3.** It is the simplest book, fully WIRED, trades 12 days a year, has about $100M capacity on the fund
   route, and already has a real-money record.
2. **Next: one of three near-equal books, chosen by style rather than by the second decimal.**
   - **Monthly only: G4.** 24% CAGR, 17 trading days, $50M. It needs MOSAIC wired. Its weak spot is 2022-26
     (Sharpe 1.20).
   - **Smoothest: G3 + capsule.** Worst year -2.5%. It needs the industry-ETF DV2 pod through a paper test. That
     pod is research tier, uses today's ETF list, and its $50M liquidity screen was chosen after the fact. It also
     needs DV2 and HPI liquidity floors to grow past $10M.
   - **In between: ladder_4.** It needs MOSAIC plus the daily MR pods.
3. **Maximum growth, about 25%:** ladder_4_1n. Drift is 25.0% with -16.5%; annual reset is 24.4% with -16.9%.
   G3-1N + capsule (23.1%, -15.0%) needs no MOSAIC. G3-1N alone (26.6%, -19.3%) sits on the Sharpe line.

## Limits

- **All backtest.** 2008 rests on synthetic instruments, validated on 2011-26 but optimistic by about 1-2 pp a year
  on the leveraged ETF out of sample. The replica ignores the 2008 short-sale ban and borrow spikes.
- **Post-result decisions (disclosed):** A2 and A3. k and the drags are fitted only on data after each fund existed.
- **Labels:** GICS labels are current (look-ahead in labels only). Membership is point-in-time.
- **Grid, not search:** the 13 books are a declared grid. The ladder weights were set earlier on the same history,
  and the industry-ETF leg was selected on the same history the day before.
- **Noise:** gate misses of 0.003-0.004 are noise, since a 4.6-year Sharpe has a standard error near 0.5. The top of
  the ordering is a tie band.
- **Data gaps:** 3-13 orders per book lack liquidity data and are left out of the capacity gates. The "2008" figures
  start on 2008-03-04.
