# QPI at the same-day close — verdict (2026-10-02)

Research-only. Nothing live changed. Frozen plan: [`scripts/research/qpi_moc_20261002/SPEC_FROZEN.md`](../../scripts/research/qpi_moc_20261002/SPEC_FROZEN.md).
Trigger: Quantitativo, "Murphy's Law" (9 Dec 2025) reports that entering QPI trades at the close instead of the
next open lifts the average trade from +0.56% to +0.64% (t 3.37).

## Verdict

**The article's gain is real only when the final close is known. With a real 15:45 decision it reverses.**
The pre-registered gate fails: 15:45 entries are 0.17 Sharpe below the next open and worse in both halves.

| Mode, 2016-01 → 2026-09 (engine costs) | CAGR | Sharpe | Max DD | Avg trade | Sharpe +5 bps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 next open (today) | 18.5% | 1.13 | −19.2% | +0.58% | 0.94 |
| M1 close, final close known (article) | 19.2% | 1.12 | −16.9% | +0.63% | 0.95 |
| **M2 close, 15:45 decision** | **15.5%** | **0.96** | **−23.8%** | **+0.53%** | **0.79** |
| M3 entries and exits at close, final known | 21.2% | 1.26 | −15.6% | +0.65% | 1.07 |
| M4 entries and exits at close, 15:45 decision | 16.0% | 0.97 | −20.9% | +0.50% | 0.78 |

Halves (M2 − M0 Sharpe): 2016–20 −0.27, 2021–26 −0.06.

## Why

- **The article's number reproduces.** Our replica gives +0.57% per trade at the next open and +0.63% at a known
  close (article: +0.56% → +0.64%), 2004–2026. At book level the known-close gain is small anyway
  (Sharpe 1.02 → 1.05, 2004–2026).
- **The signal is decided in the last 15 minutes.** Only 1,228 of the 3,100 known-close entries are also triggered
  at 15:45. Trades only the final close sees average +0.67%; trades only 15:45 sees average +0.50%.
  The IBS < 0.10 filter ("closed at the low") is the most close-sensitive input there is.
- Same mechanism as DV2 ([DV2 deep research](DV2_DEEP_RESEARCH_20260925.md), MOC section).

## Method

- Replica of `strategy_mr_qpi_ibs_rsi_exit` (QPI < 30, Close > SMA200, r3 < 0, IBS < 0.10; exit IBS > 0.9 or
  RSI2 > 90; 10 slots; turnover rank; 2.5 bps + $0.005/share, $1 minimum). QPI equals `alpha.indicators.qp_indicator`
  to 1e-14. Entry overlap with the current engine 2004–2007: 87% (path-dependent drift after the first difference).
- 15:45 state: Alpaca SIP bars ending by 15:45, mapped to Norgate by ratio to the official close (DV2 layer-2 method);
  QPI, SMA200, r3, IBS and RSI2 recomputed with today's value replaced; the turnover rank uses the previous session.
  Downloaded 2,580 extra sessions for QPI candidates. Entry coverage 96%; the rest keep the final state, which
  flatters the 15:45 modes, so the verdict cannot flip.
- M1r (known close, previous-day rank) isolates the rank change: 1.21, so M2's loss is the 15:45 information, not the rank.

## Exploratory (post-result, not promoted)

Ranking next-open candidates by the previous session's turnover instead of today's: Sharpe 1.13 → 1.16 (2016–26),
1.02 → 1.07 (2004–26). Today's turnover favours volume-spike (news) days. Small; a candidate for a frozen test only.
