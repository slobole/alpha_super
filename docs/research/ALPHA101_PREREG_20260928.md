## 0. What is already known (disclosed before freezing)

- **What the paper reports.** All figures are gross of every cost, for 2010-01-04..2013-12-31, over a universe of the
  2,000 most liquid US stocks, for dollar-neutral portfolios (paper Table 1, per alpha):

  | Statistic | Q1 | Median | Q3 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Sharpe | 1.93 | 2.22 | 2.50 |
  | Daily turnover (dollars traded / gross) | 0.34 | 0.48 | 0.65 |
  | Holding days | 1.5 | 2.1 | 2.9 |
  | Cents per share traded | 0.31 | 0.40 | 0.51 |
  | Annual return on gross dollars | 4.4% | 5.4% | 6.3% |

  - Mean pairwise correlation 15.9%.
  - Four alphas are delay-0 (42, 48, 53, 54), traded at the close of the data day.
  - The paper says the alphas are meant to be combined into one "mega-alpha" whose trades cross internally, and that
    those crossing savings are "crucial for profitability".
- **Cost arithmetic.**
  - Median gross edge per dollar traded ≈ 5.44% / (252 x 0.475) ≈ 4.5 bps.
  - The engine's cost per dollar traded is 2.5 bps slippage plus $0.005 a share (about 0.3-1.0 bps at $50-150) - about
    3-3.5 bps per side, or 8-8.5 bps under the +5 bps stress.
  - The commission alone, 0.5 cents a share, exceeds the median alpha's gross 0.40 cents a share (at the alphas' own
    share-price mix).
  - So a typical single alpha nets about zero to +1.5 bps per dollar traded at engine costs, and that is before three
    haircuts: decay since 2013; the long-only restriction; and the S&P 500 being narrower and more efficient than 2,000
    stocks.
  - Only a combination can plausibly clear costs. With 100 alphas at correlation 0.16 the effective count is about
    100 / (1 + 99 x 0.16) ≈ 5.9.
- **Earlier results in this repo.**
  - Short-term mean reversion beyond the live DV2 rule found no stock cell that passes (0 of 3,360).
  - The book already holds a short-horizon mean-reversion pod (sleeve `dv2`).
  - Book controls (official pod model, annual reset; slot = TAA 0.5, L 0.25, X 0.25):

    | Slot | 2008-11 | 2012-21 | 2022-26 | 2012-26 Sharpe / MaxDD | 2008-26 Sharpe / MaxDD |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | none (G3) | 0.689 | 1.303 | 1.257 | 1.288 / -14.1% | 1.167 / -15.3% |
    | BIL (T-bills, total return) = C_BIL | 0.728 | 1.334 | 1.437 | 1.365 / -11.7% | 1.226 / -14.3% |
- **Lead's prior, stated before any result.**
  - Gross predictive power: positive and strongest in 2000-2011.
  - Single alphas: mostly below cost after 2012.
  - Long-only pod: fails R1 in G-P2 or G-P3.
  - Probability that any candidate passes R1-R5: about 10%.
- No alpha value, IC or pod result had been computed on our data when this file was frozen.

## 1. Questions

- **Q1 (screen).** Do the paper's alphas predict next-session returns in our data, per alpha and combined? How much
  survives the owner's costs?
- **Q2 (pod).** Can a pod built from the combination beat T-bills in the book's quarter slot under the frozen rule
  (section 10)?
- **Q3 (beyond reversal).** Does the combination add anything beyond plain short-term reversal?

## 2. Hard constraints

- Causal features only; decisions at the close of t, fills at the open of t+1.
- Point-in-time (PIT) membership; delisted names are included.
- Engine semantics with historical share units:
  - 2.5 bps slippage per side;
  - $0.005 a share, $1 minimum;
  - dividend ledger at 75% net;
  - terminal liquidation at the last close.
- Long-only. SH, the -1x S&P 500 ETF, may be held long in the HEDGED form.
- The idle-cash sweep into BIL total return is the primary evaluation. Results without the sweep are reported
  alongside. The sweep is defined in `new_pod_search_20260927.common.sweep_return_ser`.
- No change to live pods, releases, schedulers, broker code or WIRED strategy files.

## 4. Inputs and point-in-time price basis

- **Price and volume inputs.**
  - `returns` = Close_t / Close_{t-1} - 1.
  - `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume` come from the CAPITALSPECIAL pull.
  - `vwap` = Turnover / Volume, clipped into [Low, High]. If Turnover or Volume is missing or not positive, `vwap` =
    (High + Low + Close) / 3. Both counts are reported.
- **`adv{d}` = mean share Volume over the last d sessions, including t (d valid values required).**
  - Deviation from the paper's wording ("dollar volume"). The formulas compare adv with share volume and form
    volume/adv ratios, which are only unit-consistent with share volume.
  - Disclosed.
- **Excluded:** alpha 56 needs market cap, which is not available point-in-time. The family is the other 100 alphas.
- **Point-in-time (PIT) conversion (anti-leak).** Back-adjusted price levels would leak future splits into
  cross-sectional ranks; this was the 26 Sep lesson.
  - Every sub-expression carries its degree of homogeneity in price, p, and in volume, v. Raw Turnover and every ratio
    have degree 0.
  - Before a cross-sectional operator (rank, scale, indneutralize), each input is converted to the value a trader saw
    on that day: multiply by k_t^p x kv_t^v, where k_t = Unadjusted Close_t / Close_t and kv_t = 1 / k_t.
    - kv_t is exact for splits; special dividends affect the price factor only (disclosed).
  - The same conversion applies before any node that is not homogeneous:
    - a sum or comparison of terms of different degree;
    - a constant added to a quantity of non-zero degree;
    - log of a quantity of non-zero degree.
  - Converted values are fixed "as known that day" and are never rescaled again.
- **Operators (paper A.1), with these conventions.**
  - `rank`: cross-sectional percentile rank among U members at t with finite values; ties averaged; range (0, 1].
  - `ts_rank(x, d)`: the rank of today's value within the last d values (ties averaged), divided by d.
  - `ts_argmax` / `ts_argmin`: days ago, 0 = today (WorldQuant BRAIN convention). The paper's wording is ambiguous,
    and the alphas that use them (1, 57, 60, 96, 98, 100) are labelled.
  - `stddev`, `covariance`, `correlation`: sample (ddof 1), over the last floor(d) sessions including t. NaN when any
    value in the window is missing or either input is constant.
  - Every window is floor(d) sessions, includes t, and needs floor(d) finite values.
  - `decay_linear` weights are d, d-1, ..., 1; today gets d.
  - `min` / `max` with a numeric second argument mean ts_min / ts_max; with two expressions they are elementwise.
  - Booleans are 1 / 0. ±inf becomes NaN. A NaN alpha is missing for that stock-day.
- **Delay-0 alphas** (42, 48, 53, 54) are executed at the next open like all others, so they become delay-1. They are
  labelled.
- **Formula transcription.** The formulas are transcribed from the paper's Appendix A into strings, and a parser plus
  evaluator implements them. Every transcription fix (line breaks, symbols) and every oddity kept as written (for
  example 29's "close - 1", 101's "+ .001") is listed in `amendments_and_bugs.json`.

## 5. Stage A - screen (descriptive; no candidate is chosen here)

For each of the 100 alphas, the composites (section 6) and the controls, on U1 (U2 descriptive):

- **Forward return fr_t = Open_{t+2} / Open_{t+1} - 1.** Excluded when missing.
- **Daily Spearman IC** between the alpha at t and fr_t among members with both values finite.
  - Reported: mean, Newey-West t (5 lags), and the share of positive days.
- **Decile long-short (LS).** Equal weight, top 10% minus bottom 10% by the alpha at t, held from the open of t+1 to
  the open of t+2.
  - Ties at decile boundaries are broken by a fixed pseudo-random order of symbols (seed 20260928), so each decile
    holds exactly 10% of the names.
  - Gross annual mean and Sharpe.
  - Daily turnover tau = sum |w_t - w_{t-1}| / 2: dollars traded (buys plus sells) per dollar of gross, where gross is
    2.
  - Break-even cost per side c* = mean gross daily return per unit of gross / mean tau. Compared with 3 bps (engine)
    and 8 bps (stress).
  - Net Sharpe at 3 and 8 bps per side.
- **Long-only top decile** minus the members' equal-weight return: gross mean, tau and c*.
- **Blocks:** P1, P2, P3, FULL, POST, PAPER.
- **Family summaries.**
  - Median IC and median c* per block.
  - Count of alphas with IC t > 2 per block. At 100 tests about 2-3 exceed t 2 by chance; this is stated.
  - Mean pairwise correlation of the LS returns in PAPER, against the paper's 15.9%.
- **Controls.**
  - REV1 = -returns_t.
  - REV5 = -(Close_t / Close_{t-5} - 1).
- **Beyond reversal (Q3).** C_EQ's IC after a daily cross-sectional regression of its rank score on REV5's rank score
  (the IC of the residual).
- **Overnight label.** The composites' IC against Open_{t+1} / Close_t - 1, the part that cannot be reached with
  next-open fills.

## 6. Composites

- **Alpha score.** z_{i,t}(s) = percentile rank of alpha i among U members at t with finite values, minus 0.5.
- **C_EQ (primary, no fitting).**
  - The mean of z over the alphas available for the stock-day.
  - At least 50 of the 100 must be available; otherwise the stock is not ranked that day.
- **C_WF (walk-forward).** C_WF = sum_i w_{i,Y} z_i / sum_i w_{i,Y}, over available alphas. At least half of the
  total weight must be available.
  - For calendar year Y >= 2003, w_{i,Y} = max(m_{i,Y}, 0) / sum_j max(m_{j,Y}, 0).
    - m_{i,Y} is the mean daily IC of alpha i on the same universe.
    - It covers decisions from 2000-01-03 whose fr is known by the last session of Y-1.
  - The paper's signs are kept; there is no sign flipping.
  - Years 2000-2002, and any year where every m <= 0, use C_EQ.
- **Sensitivity (label only).** C_EQ without the 18 IndNeutralize alphas (48, 58, 59, 63, 67, 69, 70, 76, 79, 80, 82,
  87, 89, 90, 91, 93, 97, 100).

## 7. Stage B - pods (candidates)

- **Rule at each close t**, on U1 members with a valid composite score:
  - **Exit** at the open of t+1 in any of these cases:
    - the held name's rank is worse than B x N;
    - it is no longer a member;
    - its score is missing.
  - **Enter:** fill free slots with the best-ranked names that are not held and rank within the top N. Score ties are
    broken by symbol.
  - **Size:** budget b = min(V_t / N, (C_t + P_t) / n_t); raw shares = trunc(b / U_t).
    - C_t is cash, P_t is the exits' value at the close of t, and n_t is the number of free slots.
  - No re-sizing. Terminal liquidation at the last close when a series ends.
- **Grid per composite:** N in {10, 20, 40} x B in {1, 2, 4, 8}. That is 12 cells, and 24 for the two composites.
- **Anchor A0:** C_EQ, N 20, B 2.
- **HEDGED form (secondary candidates).**
  - Built on the long-only candidate of each composite.
  - Stock budget b = min(0.5 x V_t / N, available cash / free slots).
  - SH is held long at 50% of NAV and re-sized to 50% at each month-end close.
  - Starts 2006-07-03, when SH exists.
- **Labels through the same machinery:** REV1 and REV5 at N 20, B 2.
- **Costs:** engine costs, and +5 bps per side stress.
- **Capital base:** $100k, as in earlier studies. Labels also at $10k, $25k and $1M, because the $1 minimum
  commission weighs on small positions.

## 9. Parity, tests and invariance (before any grid cell or Stage A number is read)

- **Engine parity.** The real engine replays the replica's intents for A0 on U1. It must match:
  - daily-return correlation >= 0.9999;
  - CAGR within 0.05 pp;
  - identical positions.
- **Unit tests.**
  - The parser: precedence, ternary, the min/max overloads, and floor of windows.
  - Every operator against a naive loop implementation, on random panels with NaNs.
  - PIT conversion on a synthetic 2:1 split.
  - The composites, the pod's buffer rule, and C_BIL reproducing the section-0 table.
- **V1 - download-date invariance** (the anti-leak test).
  - For truncation dates 2005-06-30, 2012-03-30 and 2019-09-30, rebuild U1 as a download on that date would show it:
    - data after the date dropped;
    - prices x k_T0; Volume x kv_T0;
    - Turnover and Unadjusted Close unchanged.
  - All 100 alphas must equal the full-panel values over the 300 sessions up to the date: relative 1e-9, same NaN
    pattern.
  - A mismatch is allowed only where a floating-point tie flips a comparison or a rank. Each one is listed.
- **V2 - random per-stock rescaling.** Multiply OHLC by constants in [0.1, 10] and divide Volume by the same
  constant, leaving Unadjusted Close unchanged. All alphas must be unchanged (relative 1e-9).
- **V3 - walk-forward look-ahead.** C_WF weights for each year, recomputed from data truncated at the end of the prior
  year, must equal the full-run weights.
- If anything fails, it is fixed before any Stage A figure or grid cell is read.

## 10. Candidates and decision rule (frozen; the same rule as the earlier new-pod studies)

- **Neighbourhoods.** The 3 x 3 box in (N, B), clipped. Plateau value = median over the box of standalone FULL Sharpe
  with the sweep, at engine costs.
- **Candidates.**
  - Per composite, the cell with the highest plateau value; ties go to the cell nearest A0.
  - Plus the HEDGED form of each: up to four candidates.
- **R1.** Candidate-book Sharpe strictly above C_BIL's in each of G-P1, G-P2 and G-P3.
- **R2.** Candidate-book max DD no worse than C_BIL's by more than 2.0 pp, on G-FULL and G-LONG.
- **R3.** R1 and R2 also hold with +5 bps per side on X and L.
- **R4.** The same cell and form on U2 (its own composite and weights): candidate-book G-FULL Sharpe > C_BIL's.
- **R5.** In 2021-26 the largest order stays below 5% of ADV20 up to a pod AUM of at least $5M.
- **Labels:**
  - beats G3; beats C_SPY; beats the `dv2` slot;
  - meets the owner's gates (book Sharpe 1.35, max DD -20%);
  - the POST block;
  - REV1 and REV5 pods;
  - the IndNeutralize sensitivity;
  - small-account economics at $10k and $25k, and the $1M run.
- **Confidence.**
  - Reality Check over the 24 long-only cells and 2 HEDGED cells versus C_BIL: stationary bootstrap, block 21, 2,000
    draws, seed 20260928.
  - Paired bootstrap of each candidate versus C_BIL.
  - DSR on returns in excess of BIL, with N = 503 (a lower bound: 377 earlier + 100 alphas + 24 cells + 2 HEDGED).
  - Walk-forward: candidates re-chosen on P1 standalone Sharpe only, then evaluated on P2, P3, G-P2 and G-P3.
- **If a candidate passes:** recommend a forward paper line first, never a live change.
- **If none passes:** say so. No further Alpha101 variant is tried in this session. Stage A stays descriptive
  information.

## 11. Assumptions and limitations

- A1: last-known GICS; `adv` as share volume; the ts_argmax convention; vwap from Turnover (special-dividend factors
  ignored).
- A2: The paper's figures come from a 2,000-stock, dollar-neutral combination with internal crossing and institutional
  costs. They are context, not a benchmark this test must match.
- A3: Open-to-open returns exclude the close-to-open bid-ask bounce. Close-based backtests of reversal overstate it.
- A4: P1 contains 2000-02 and 2008-09, both kind to reversal. The book blocks start in 2008 with the synthetic TAA
  proxy. There is one history.
- A5: The sweep is frictionless and daily. SH carries its expense ratio and daily reset.
