# Limit-on-close and limit-below-open for QPI and DV2 — verdict (2026-10-02)

Research-only. Nothing live changed. Frozen plan: [`scripts/research/qpi_moc_20261002/SPEC_LOC.md`](../../scripts/research/qpi_moc_20261002/SPEC_LOC.md)
(amendment L1 fixed the DV2 limit in validation, before any result). Follows [QPI at the close](QPI_MOC_20261002.md).

## Verdict

**No arm passes. Keep next-open execution for QPI and DV2.**

- **The LOC mechanism itself works.** A limit-on-close order whose limit is the highest close that still passes the
  signal, computed at 15:45, recovers most of what a known close would give: QPI 1.19 vs 1.24, DV2 0.73 vs 0.77
  (same order policy, final-state limits). It is the right tool for any future strategy that has a real
  same-close edge.
- **QPI:** LOC entries plus LOC exits reach 1.19 vs 1.13 at the next open (+0.06, below the +0.10 gate). With a
  5 bps "trade-through" fill it drops to 1.06, under the baseline. The gain depends on fills exactly at the limit.
- **DV2:** close entries do not help even with a known close (0.77 here; 1.04 vs 1.07 in the DV2 study). Nothing
  to recover.
- **Limit below the open:** adverse selection as expected. One near-miss: QPI with a 0.5–1% limit below the prior
  close, +0.04 / +0.05 (2016–26) and +0.07 / +0.09 (2004–26), but worse in 2021–26.
- **Previous-day turnover rank (QPI):** +0.02. Noise.

## Method

- Limits (exact rank formulas):
  - QPI: QPI < 30 via the 3-day return threshold, plus IBS < 0.10 on the 15:45 range.
  - DV2: DV2 < 10 with a self-consistent mid.
  - The rule holds at the limit in 100% of cases and fails 5 bps above it in 99.7–99.995%.
- Order policy: closest-to-fill first, F orders (A) or 2F (B).
- Generic runner reproduces the QPI and DV2 replicas: Sharpe 1.1300 = 1.1300; 1.0687 vs 1.0693.
- Alpaca SIP 15:45 state for every candidate and held name (two download passes).
