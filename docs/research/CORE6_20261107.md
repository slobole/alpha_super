## The rule

CORE5 stays exactly as it is: five 20% sleeves (SPY, IEF, GLD, DBC, UUP), each switched to BIL by the adaptive-average trend rule, plus the small DBC short.

On top of that, a Bitcoin overlay is taken from the BIL reserve:

```text
BTC weight = 1/6 x min(1, 10% / sigma60_BTC), only while BTC's own CORE5 switch is long
```

- On average this is 2.1% of the book, and never more than 8%.
- If all five sleeves are long and BIL cannot fund the overlay, the five are scaled down pro rata. The book never levers.
- BTC's switch and sigma60 are computed from the IBIT close at 16:00 ET, on the same clock as the five ETFs. There is no extra live data source. This is the owner's decision of 2026-11-07, see "Signal source" below.
- Rebalancing follows CORE5: any switch change, or the month end.
- Fills happen at the next open.
- BTC costs 15 bp per side.

## Independent check (2026-11-07)

- **Holding BTC all the time** at the same vol-sized weight, with no switch, gives Sharpe 1.25 and max drawdown -7.7%. The gain comes from holding about 2% BTC. The switch mainly saves about 1 pt of drawdown.
- **2012-13 carry almost half of the summed yearly gain.** 2013 alone added 17.4 pts.
- **Plan on the recent number.** Since 2021 CORE6 adds about +0.07 Sharpe and +0.9 pts CAGR a year (t 1.3), not +0.40.
- **Costs do not matter.** Even 2% a side before 2014 leaves Sharpe at 1.25. The BTC weight at the Mt.Gox collapse (Feb 2014) was about 0.85% of the book.
- **BTC is less of a diversifier now.** Its correlation with SPY rose from 0.04 (2011-16) to 0.39 (2024-26), and with CORE5 from 0.02 to 0.20.

## Caveats

- **The recent edge is small.** Most of the gain was made in 2011-20. Since 2021 it is +0.08 Sharpe and not significant; since IBIT listed it is slightly negative.
- **No untouched data.** The house has already seen all of the BTC history. ETH is the only validation.
- **The DBC short is still undecided.** The CORE5 robustness study rejected it, but the live spec keeps it. Every variant here keeps it too.
- **BTC history before IBIT comes from Pakal's cache.** The spec reads it from `research_cache/core6_qualification_study/btc_unit.parquet`. Before 2017-08 BTC fills at the end-of-day price, which is later and conservative. From 2024-01-11 the live spec uses IBIT from Norgate, so new days need no cache refresh.
