Calm and stressAll history, weekly

Every week is calm or stress, by what QQQ did. Stress has far fewer weeks, so its correlations are noisier, and they lean high by construction when markets swing more. A strategy with a short history sees only the stress weeks it lived through: the tick on its bar is QQQ in those same weeks. The pale band around a stress dot is its noise: a calm dot inside it has not really moved. Worst weeks together is free of that bias: it counts weeks, not correlation.
