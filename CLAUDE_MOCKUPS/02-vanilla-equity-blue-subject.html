Equity curve — ink subject vs blue subject

Same data, same layout, one change: the strategy line takes the brand blue that the rest of the system already uses for “the subject”. The benchmark stays neutral slate. Everything else — hairlines, Plex, red reserved for losses — is unchanged.

A · Current — ink subject
Strategy is ink, benchmark is slate. Documentary, but the one chart an investor remembers has no brand in it — and at small sizes the two dark lines converge.
Ink works in print and in the numbered-plate report language.
B · Proposal — blue subject
Strategy takes the brand blue used by links, active tabs and the first analytic series. “The blue line is us” becomes true on every surface: Bench, reports, live console.
One rule everywhere: blue = the thing we run, grey = the world, red = only losses.
